## Create your first agent

Learn how to create and deploy your first AI phone agent in just a few steps.

### Initial Setup
From your dashboard:
1. Click "Create New Agent"
2. Give your agent a name
3. Give your agent some initial instructions
4. Click "Create Agent"


### Configure Your Agent

#### Set up agent behavior
Configure how your agent thinks and responds:
1. Define your agent's personality and tone
2. Add your business's key information
3. Set up specific instructions for handling common scenarios
4. Configure business hours and availability

#### Connect a phone number
Get your agent ready to take calls:
1. We'll provide you with a testing number to start
2. You can also connect your own phone number to your agent

### Test and Go Live

#### Test your agent
Before going live:
1. Call your number and try out different conversation scenarios
2. Review call recordings and transcripts
3. Adjust agent settings as needed

#### Launch your agent
Ready to go live?
1. Review and finalize your agent's configuration
2. Set up a production phone number
3. Monitor your first few calls
4. Configure any needed adjustments
